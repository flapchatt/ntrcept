An arcade-style baseball game with pitch timing, balls and strikes, base runners, innings, all 30 MLB clubs, and a single-elimination tournament.

You bat in the top half and pitch in the bottom half. Timing matters.

How to play

01
Watch the pitch

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Tap Space at the right moment to swing, pitch, or dive on defense.

02
Play the count

Four balls means a walk. Three strikes means an out. Three outs ends a half-inning.

03
Run the bases

Singles, doubles, triples, home runs, walks, and outs affect base runners and runs.

On the mound

1–4
Choose your pitch

Fastball, curveball, changeup, or slider. Aim with the 3×3 target grid.

↵
Throw it

Use the pitch button or press Enter. The opposing batter picks their own swings.

30

All current MLB clubs included.
Quick games let you choose an opponent. Tournament mode seeds every club into a bracket with two first-round byes.
