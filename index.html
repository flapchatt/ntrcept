New to Flapchat?

Flapchat requires an active connection to its network to sign in or create accounts. The app blocks access when the network service is unreachable. Account data saves locally; globally reserved usernames and offline account discovery need a shared account registry.

Connection required

Flapchat needs an internet connection. Sign-in and account creation also require the Flapchat signaling service; if that service is reconnecting, the app will show its status without blocking the whole screen when the internet is available.
